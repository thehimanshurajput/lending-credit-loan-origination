# Banking Business Analyst | Lending & Credit

Independent portfolio case study demonstrating business analysis for a loan origination system (LOS) enhancement. The exercise focuses on adding co-applicants and supporting consistent eligibility review.

## Portfolio scope

This is a learning and design exercise based on a generalized lending scenario. It does not represent banking employment, client delivery, production implementation, or executed testing. The business rules and thresholds are illustrative scenario assumptions, not lender policy or regulatory advice.

## Project: LOS co-applicant enhancement

The case study covers:

- Adding co-applicants and capturing required applicant details
- Calculating combined income, existing obligations, and debt-to-income (DTI)
- Retrieving credit bureau information for each applicant
- Routing exceptions and failed bureau requests for review
- Presenting consolidated application data for an underwriter decision
